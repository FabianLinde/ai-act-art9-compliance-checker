# AI Act Art.9 Compliance Checker
Compliance Checker for the Risk Management System required by Art. 9 of the EU AI Act.

Offers verification of a semantic risk management model against the requirements of Article 9 of the EU AI Act. 
This tool uses SHACL constraints defined in `rules.shacl` to validate your model's compliance. 
Users can upload your own Turtle file or use the interactive sample below.

Find the Streamlit app here: https://ai-act-art9-compliance-checker.streamlit.app/
